Understand the Dimensions

Clarity

Think it through ↔ Talk it through

The clarity dimension describes the process through which ideas become clear, including how much someone relies on private reflection, active conversation, questions, and social exchange to develop their thinking.

Navigation

Find the path ↔ Map the path

The navigation dimension describes how someone responds to uncertainty when beginning work, including their comfort with discovering direction through action and their preference for establishing structure before moving forward.

Scope

Go deeper ↔ Go wider

The scope dimension describes how someone directs attention when exploring an idea or problem, including the balance between sustained depth and examining a broader range of perspectives, possibilities, and connections.

Expression

Understated ↔ Expressive

The expression dimension describes how someone manages and communicates their inner responses, including how deliberately they regulate expression and how readily their opinions, preferences, reactions, and energy become visible.

Work Type Privacy Notice

A Work Type—not a conversation record.

This notice explains how MEMOVA uses high-level patterns to generate your Work Type, and what happens to the conversation history used as the source.

01

Patterns, not transcripts

The Work Type method is intended to summarize high-level tendencies in how you think, navigate uncertainty, focus attention, and express ideas. A result should not reproduce private prompts, responses, or sensitive excerpts.

02

De-identified by default

Names, account details, employers, contact information, and direct quotations are not needed to explore your Work Type and should be excluded from a saved result.

03

No extra archive on this page

This standalone page does not upload or archive your conversation history. It contains no account system, submission form, analytics, tracking pixels, or external network requests.

04

You control what you share

You may share the resulting archetype and personal user manual when you choose. Do not include source conversations—or another person’s information—without permission.

When MEMOVA analyzes your Work Type

MEMOVA uses the conversation history made available for this purpose to identify high-level work patterns and generate your Work Type and personal user manual. The saved result is designed to contain the pattern summary—not a copy of the source conversations.

How this result should be used

Work Types are for personal reflection, learning, and better collaboration. They should never be used for diagnosis, hiring, ranking, eligibility decisions, or exclusion.

Share thoughtfully

You do not need to provide additional confidential, medical, financial, or personally identifying information to explore these types. This notice describes MEMOVA’s Work Type experience. Any original conversations that remain stored in another service continue to follow that service’s own privacy and retention settings.
