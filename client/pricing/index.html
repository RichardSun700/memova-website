GOOD TO KNOW

A few details before you start.

How does the free recording allowance work?+

Free includes 300 recording minutes per month, shared across Meeting and Spark. Your allowance resets each month.

How many webpages can I create?+

Free includes 10 webpages per month. Pro has no monthly page limit. Both plans turn your conversations and notes into webpages you can share.

How does Meeting AI Report work?+

Meeting AI Report is off by default. Turn it on in the app to let AI capture excerpts from your meeting conversation and correct the transcript in real time. The feature is included with Pro. Free users can join our community for trial access.

How long can a recording be?+

On both Free and Pro, each Meeting or Spark recording can last up to 4 hours.

Where do I upgrade to Pro?+

Upgrade in the Memova app. If you haven’t installed Memova yet, download it from the App Store first.
